How to delete your data

Last updated October 7, 2026

Disconnect in Hooklyn

Open Hooklyn, go to Settings, Auto-post, and tap Disconnect next to the account. Its saved access token is deleted right away.

Remove access on the platform

  • Google and YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose Hooklyn, then Remove access.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions, choose Hooklyn, then Remove.
  • Instagram: in Instagram, Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites, choose Hooklyn, then Remove.

Ask for full deletion

Email hooklyn350@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the account name you connected. Your tokens, account details and posting history will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a reply confirming it.

Videos already posted to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram stay on your account until you delete them there.
