Privacy policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

This policy explains what Hooklyn collects, why, and how you can remove it. Hooklyn runs on its owner's own device. There is no Hooklyn server that holds your data.

What Hooklyn collects

  • Connected accounts. When you connect YouTube, TikTok or Instagram, you sign in on that platform's own page. Hooklyn never sees your password. It receives an access token, plus your channel or account name and ID, so it can upload videos for you.
  • Videos and clips. The videos you give Hooklyn and the clips it makes, including captions and titles.
  • Posting history. Which clips were posted, where, when, and the resulting video link.
  • Your settings and clip ratings. For example caption style, posts per day, and thumbs up or down on clips.

Hooklyn does not collect payment details, contacts, location, or anything from people who watch your videos.

How Hooklyn uses it

  • To upload clips you choose, or that you set to post automatically, to your own connected accounts.
  • To show you what was posted and whether it worked.
  • To pick better clips over time, based on your own ratings.

Hooklyn does not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone except the services listed below, and only to do the job you asked for.

Services Hooklyn uses

  • YouTube. Hooklyn uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and read public trending videos. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles your data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok and Instagram, when connected, under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Anthropic (Claude), only if the owner turns on the optional Claude picker. Then the transcript of a video is sent to Anthropic to choose the best moments. It is not used to train models under Anthropic's API terms.

Google user data

Hooklyn's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to upload and manage videos on your own channel, never for ads, and is never transferred to anyone else.

Where it is stored and for how long

Everything is stored in a private folder on the owner's device. Access tokens are kept until you disconnect the account. Clips and posting history are kept until the owner deletes them.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any account in Hooklyn under Settings, Auto-post. This deletes its saved token.
  • Remove Hooklyn's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or in TikTok or Instagram's own app settings.
  • Ask for anything to be deleted. See How to delete your data.

Children

Hooklyn is not meant for anyone under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
