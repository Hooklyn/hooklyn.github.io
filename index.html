Hooklyn

Hooklyn turns long videos into short vertical clips with captions, then posts them to the creator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Hooklyn is a personal tool run by its owner. It only posts to accounts the owner connects through each platform's official sign-in, and only clips the owner has permission to use.
